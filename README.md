# Enterprise Reasoning

An open initiative to develop a vendor-neutral standard for representing reasoning across people, software, and AI.

**Start here:** [Read the white paper](Enterprise-Reasoning-White-Paper-2026-09.pdf) · [Participate](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Decision process](GOVERNANCE.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

## What we are building

The September 2026 white paper proposes structured reasoning, human–AI collaboration, lifecycle management, reasoning across information sources, evolving semantics, and attribution to decisions and outcomes. It provides the starting point for community discussion and specification development.

**Current stage: white paper and community formation.** No normative specification, approved schema, or conformance certification has been adopted. Examples in the white paper are illustrative.

## Join the work

You do not need organization membership to participate in this public repository. With a GitHub account, you can ask a question, describe a use case, review a proposal, or suggest an edit.

1. Read the white paper and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
2. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/issues/new) with a question, use case, or proposal. Cite the relevant paper section where possible.
3. Discuss the proposal openly; use a pull request when there is concrete text to review.
4. Follow the [decision process](GOVERNANCE.md) to understand how proposals enter a working draft.

Business domain expertise, interoperability experience, editorial review, and implementation feedback are all welcome. Technical proposals should be evaluated on their merits, regardless of employer or tool vendor.

## Rights and policy status

Publication here does not add a new license to the white paper. Specification and code licenses, patent policy, and any formal contributor agreement remain founding decisions to be published. Do not assume that an open-standard objective itself grants implementation or redistribution rights. Initial participation can focus on discussion, use cases, and review while these policies are resolved.
